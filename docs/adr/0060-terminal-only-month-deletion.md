# Terminal-Only Month Deletion

Users may only delete either the chronologically latest month (to roll back or cancel an unneeded planning cycle) or the chronologically earliest month (to prune tail history). Deleting intermediate past months situated between other recorded months is strictly prohibited.

Allowing intermediate months to be deleted would introduce chronological gaps in account balance histories, orphaned rollover references, and corrupted multi-account timeline trajectories. Restricting deletion to the historical boundaries guarantees unbroken ledger continuity.
