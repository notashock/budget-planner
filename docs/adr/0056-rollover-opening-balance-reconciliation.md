# Rollover Opening Balance Reconciliation

When rolling over a budget month into the next month with unpaid one-time items carried forward, the opening balance of the new month adds back the amounts of those rolled-forward unpaid items to the previous month's ending balance.

Without this reconciliation, carrying forward the projected ending balance while simultaneously copying unpaid items as pending expenses in the new month double-penalizes the user by deducting unfulfilled expenses twice.
